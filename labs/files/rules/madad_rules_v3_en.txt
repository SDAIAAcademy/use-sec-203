Rules of the Madad monthly household support programme
Version 3 (in force). This version repeals and replaces version 2.
Kanaf Social Support Agency

Clause 1: Purpose
Madad is a monthly cash support for low-income households. The programme covers no other kind of support.

Clause 2: Who applies
The head of the household, a man or a woman, applies. The applicant must reside in the fictional kingdom of this case and be 18 or older.

Clause 3: Income limit
A household qualifies when its monthly income does not exceed SAR 1,300 per household member.

Clause 4: Amount
SAR 400 for the applicant and SAR 250 for each dependant, up to SAR 2,400 a month.

Clause 5: Documents
Identity document, family record, proof of income for the last 3 months, and a bank account number (IBAN) in the applicant's name.

Clause 6: Applying
Applications are made through the portal or at service centres. There is no application fee.

Clause 7: Decision time
A decision is issued within 20 working days of a complete application.

Clause 8: Payment
Support is paid on the tenth day of each Gregorian month.

Clause 9: Reporting a change
A beneficiary reports any change in income or in the number of household members within 30 days.

Clause 10: Annual update
Household data are updated every 12 months. Payment is put on hold for a beneficiary who has not updated within 60 days of being notified, until the update is made.

Clause 11: Stopping support
Support is stopped or held only by a decision of a competent officer, after the beneficiary is notified and given 15 days to respond.

Clause 12: Appeals
A beneficiary may appeal any decision within 60 days of being notified of it. The grievances committee decides within 30 working days.

Clause 13: Recovery
Amounts paid in error are recovered in instalments of no more than 10% of the monthly support.

Clause 14: Help channels
The call centre and the automated assistant Bashir. Bashir answers general questions from these rules. It does not see case files and does not decide applications. Individual cases, complaints, appeals and reports go to an officer.

A synthetic document for training at SDAIA Academy. The agency, the programme, the rules, the systems, the people and the figures are all invented.
